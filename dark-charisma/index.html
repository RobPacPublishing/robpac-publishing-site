The Dark Side of Charisma

How Narcissists Use Charisma to Manipulate

by Ryan Walker

About This Book

A book exploring the dark side of charisma, revealing how charm and influence can be weaponized for manipulation. It offers tools to identify emotional manipulation tactics used by charismatic narcissists in both professional and personal settings. The guide teaches readers to recognize signs of manipulation like gaslighting, defend themselves effectively, and use their own charisma ethically. Essential reading for anyone looking to protect themselves from manipulators and develop authentic relationships built on trust.
