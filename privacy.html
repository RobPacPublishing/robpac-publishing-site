When you use the free eBook request form, we collect the name and email address you voluntarily provide, the title of the requested eBook, and your acknowledgement of this Privacy Policy. The information entered in the request form is processed solely to receive, manage, and respond to your request. Where applicable, personal data may therefore be processed in countries whose data-protection framework differs from that of the European Union. Such processing remains subject to the safeguards and contractual arrangements adopted by the relevant provider.

6. Retention

Personal data submitted through the form is kept only for the time reasonably necessary to handle the request, respond to related communications, and comply with any applicable legal obligations. Data that is no longer required will be deleted or anonymised.

Subject to the conditions established by applicable law, you may request access to your personal data, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, data portability, or withdrawal of consent. Requests may be sent to robpacpublishing@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority, including the Italian Data Protection Authority where applicable.
